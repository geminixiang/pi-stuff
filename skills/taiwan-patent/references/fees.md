# TIPO official fees

Source: 《專利規費收費辦法》 https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL011253
List: https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/patents/482.html
Reduction: 《專利年費減免辦法》 https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL011256

Amounts are New Taiwan Dollars. Recheck the 辦法 if a lookup is after a fee revision.

## Filing and certificate

| Item | Amount | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 申請發明 | 3,500 | electronic −600 |
| 申請新型 / 設計 | 3,000 | electronic −600 |
| 專利證書費 | 1,000 | paid at grant |
| 新型技術報告（≤10 claims） | 5,000 | +600 per extra claim |

There is no natural-person discount on the application fee in the current 收費辦法. Electronic filing is the usual 600 reduction.

## Annual fees — 新型 (10-year term from filing)

| Years | Full | 自然人 / 學校 / 中小企業 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | 2,500 | 1,700 (−800) |
| 4–6 | 4,000 | 2,800 (−1,200) |
| 7–10 | 8,000 | 8,000 (no cut) |

Natural-person 10-year government total ≈ 申請 2,400 + 證書 1,000 + 年費 45,500 = **48,900**.

## Timing

- Year 1 is due at grant (`licence-date`).
- Later years are due by `charge-expir-date`.
- Unpaid: 6-month grace with surcharge, then the right lapses from the day after the original due date.
- Agent drafting for a simple 新型 is typically 10,000–15,000 once; optional annuity handling 500–1,000/year. Those are market rates, not official fees.
